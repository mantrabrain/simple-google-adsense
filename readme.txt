= External services =

AdFlow connects to these services only when you use the related feature:

* **Google AdSense** - when ads are enabled, visitors' browsers load `https://pagead2.googlesyndication.com/pagead/js/adsbygoogle.js`, which requests and shows ads. Google processes visitor data under its own terms: [Google AdSense terms](https://www.google.com/adsense/new/localized-terms), [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), [How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
* **Google Funding Choices / consent messages** - only if you use Google's own consent message in AdSense; AdFlow waits for it and does not load it itself.
* **Your own site** - the ads.txt checker requests your site's public `/ads.txt` URL to confirm it is reachable. No data is sent anywhere else.
* **Custom code units** load whatever third-party code you paste into them; that network's terms apply.

AdFlow's own statistics are stored in your WordPress database as daily totals. They use no cookies and store no IP addresses or visitor identifiers.

== Installation ==

1. In your dashboard go to **Plugins → Add New**, search for **AdFlow** and click **Install Now**, then **Activate**. (Or upload the ZIP under **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin**.)
2. Open **AdFlow → Settings** and paste your AdSense **Publisher ID** (it starts with `pub-`; [where to find it](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/105516)).
3. Turn on **Auto Ads**, or create an ad unit under **AdFlow → Ad Units** and switch on a placement under **AdFlow → Placements**.
4. Turn on **AdFlow → Settings → ads.txt** so AdSense stops warning "Earnings at risk".
5. Check the **Dashboard**: the setup checklist and site health show anything left to do.

Ads usually start showing within minutes; on a new AdSense site Auto Ads can take up to 48 hours.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Is AdFlow free? =

Yes. The free plugin on WordPress.org has no limit on ads, placements or sites and adds no branding to your ads. [AdFlow Pro](https://matrixaddons.com/plugins/adflow/?utm_source=wporg&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=faq) is a separate plugin for earnings reports, click protection, advanced targeting and selling ads.

= Does AdFlow help me get approved for AdSense? =

No plugin can. You apply at [adsense.google.com](https://adsense.google.com/). Once approved, AdFlow adds your code, ads.txt and ads in minutes.

= Why are my ads not showing? =

While logged in, open a page and click **AdFlow → Inspect ads on this page** in the toolbar. Each ad is labelled Filled, Unfilled (Google had no ad - common on new or low-traffic sites), Requested (waiting for Google) or Not requested (AdSense script blocked by an ad blocker, lazy loading, or hidden by a targeting rule). Then check **Site health** on the AdFlow Dashboard. Remember that ads are hidden from logged-in administrators if you turned that option on.

= What is ads.txt and how do I fix "Earnings at risk"? =

ads.txt is a public file that says who may sell ads on your site. Turn it on under **AdFlow → Settings → ads.txt** and AdFlow serves the correct AdSense line automatically; click **Check now** to confirm Google can read it. The warning in AdSense clears after Google recrawls the file, usually within a few days.

= Can I insert ads automatically without shortcodes? =

Yes. Create an ad unit under **AdFlow → Ad Units**, then enable a placement (before content, after paragraph N, after content) under **AdFlow → Placements**. Pro adds mid-article, between posts, before comments, sticky and WooCommerce placements.

= Can I use Auto Ads and manual ads together? =

Yes. Many publishers use Auto Ads plus a few fixed units in their best positions. You can also exclude Auto Ads from chosen post types or pages.

= Does AdFlow work with other ad networks and Google Ad Manager? =

Yes. Paste any network's code into a **Custom code** ad unit (Google Ad Manager, Media.net, Ezoic and others) and place it like any other ad. AdFlow Pro adds native Google Ad Manager (GPT) units with sizes and key-value targeting.

= Is AdFlow GDPR-compliant? =

AdFlow supports Google Consent Mode v2 ("denied" by default in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) and can load ads only after consent from a Google-certified IAB TCF v2.2 message or a consent plugin (Complianz, CookieYes, Cookiebot or any WP Consent API plugin). Its own statistics use no cookies and store no IP addresses.

= Does it work with page caching and CDNs? =

Yes. Rotation, statistics, device and traffic rules (and Pro's country targeting and frequency caps) run in the browser or read a CDN header, so they keep working with any page cache.

= Can I show my own banners or sell ad space? =

Yes. Create an ad unit of type **Image banner** or **Text ad**, add the link and optional start and end dates. It gets a "Sponsored" label and a `rel="sponsored"` link by default, and impressions and clicks appear under **AdFlow → Reports**. [AdFlow Pro](https://matrixaddons.com/plugins/adflow/?utm_source=wporg&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=faq-sell) adds a self-serve "Advertise with us" page, advertiser report links and emails.

= Can I switch from Advanced Ads, Ad Inserter, AdRotate or WP QUADS? =

Yes, under **AdFlow → Settings → Switch to AdFlow**. Ads, groups, placements and (AdRotate and WP QUADS) statistics are imported; the old plugin can then be deactivated and its shortcodes keep working.

= Will AdFlow slow down my site? =

No. Its script and styles load only on pages that show an ad, the front-end script is small and deferred, and Pro adds lazy loading with reserved height so ads cause no layout shift.

= I upgraded from an older version. Do I need to change anything? =

No. All shortcodes (`[adsense]`, `[adsense_banner]`, `[adsense_inarticle]`, `[adsense_infeed]`, `[adsense_multiplex]` and the older `[adsense_matched_content]`), blocks and settings keep working with unchanged output. New features stay off until you turn them on.

= Which shortcodes are available? =

`[adflow id="123"]` shows a saved ad unit (recommended). For a single AdSense unit: `[adsense ad_slot="1234567890"]`, `[adsense_banner]`, `[adsense_inarticle]`, `[adsense_infeed layout_key="..."]` and `[adsense_multiplex]`. The **AdFlow Ad** block does the same visually.

= Where can I get help? =

Read **AdFlow → Docs** in your dashboard, or ask in the [support forum](https://wordpress.org/support/plugin/simple-google-adsense/). AdFlow Pro customers get priority email support.
